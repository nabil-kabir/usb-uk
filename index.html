Questions

Before you ask.

Is it genuine?

Yes. Every perfume we sell is an original, in the maker's sealed box. Ask us for a photo of the exact box before you order.

I can't smell it online. What if I pick wrong?

Tell us what you wear now, or what you want it for. We will point you to scents that match, so you are not buying blind.

Is the price on the card final?

It is a guide. Prices move with stock, so we confirm today's price on WhatsApp before you order.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery. You pay when the parcel reaches you.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in Bangladesh. Tell us your area and we will tell you how long it takes before you order.

Do you sell testers?

Sometimes. A tester is the same perfume in a plain box. We always tell you before you buy.
